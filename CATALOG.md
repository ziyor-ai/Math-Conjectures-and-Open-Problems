# Complete Top 100 Catalog

Source edition: October 24, 2026. This English translation follows the contents on pages 5–9 of the [Chinese PDF](AI_Top_100_Mathematical_Conjectures_CN.pdf). Rankings, status labels, and page numbers are preserved from that edition; the labels are not an independent assessment of current research status. See page 4 of the PDF for the original status definitions.

## Status Legend

| Label | Meaning in the source edition |
| --- | --- |
| Open | The general version used in the publication was not confirmed to be resolved; partial work may exist. |
| Partial results | Important special cases, conditional results, or version-dependent results exist; these do not settle the general statement. |
| Recent claims under review | Recent proof or counterexample claims are discussed; consult the entry for their scope and verification status. |
| Disputed proof claim | A proof has been claimed, but the publication does not confirm that substantive disputes have been resolved. |
| Recent proof literature | Recent proofs and explanatory literature are cited; the historical ranking is retained, and the publication does not independently reprove the result. |
